But for good reason. I've been working hard behind the scenes to make it better and more effective for you.
Brief generation
The brief generator got smarter. The writing reads more like a real supervisor wrote it, and it never repeats the same scene twice. You can generate practice briefs for three category types: Brands, Film, and Games.
Brief types
Catalog briefs are the ones you submit to. There are two kinds. Community briefs, generated by composers like you and great for practicing. And Sonant briefs, curated around tracks the catalog is actively looking for.

But wait, there's more, haha. A new category: Client briefs. These are real paid sync jobs from inside our closed network. Real win fees, sometimes demo fees. Access is reserved for composers who earn the Verified Composer checkmark, which takes 3 tracks accepted into the catalog. Which brings us to the next topic.
Upload vs submit
This one confused people, so let me try to clear it up.
Upload The free option. MP3, public or private, and it just lives on that brief's playlist and on your profile. Good for practicing out loud, but it doesn't go to the catalog.
Submit The real one. It costs a credit, you send an MP3 or WAV, and it comes privately to us. You'll get written feedback every time, and if it's strong we put it in the catalog and pitch it around. Land 3 accepted tracks and you're verified, which unlocks the client briefs.
You get 1 submission credit a month just for having an account, and you can buy more if you need them. Client briefs don't have a playlist, by the way. Those are actual jobs, so they come straight to us.
Written vs live feedback
There are two ways to get notes on your stuff.

Written feedback just comes automatically with every submission, whether we end up taking the track or not.

Live feedback is a session, basically 45 minutes where we listen together, hold it up against the brief, and talk through what it'd take to place. Good timing for it is before you submit, after you've gotten written notes, or honestly anytime a mix has been sitting open too long and you need a second set of ears. Sessions are $50.
So what should you actually do?
Generate a brief, write to it like it's a real job, upload a take if you want it up on the playlist, and submit your best one when it's ready. Or just book a session if you want live ears on something you're stuck on.
