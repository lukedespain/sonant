03 · Upload and submit
Two different moves.
This was the confusing one. It is clean now.
Upload
Free. MP3. Public or private. Lives on the brief playlist and on your profile. Does not go to the catalog.
Submit
One credit. MP3 or WAV. Private to the team. Written feedback. If it is strong, we place it and pitch it. Three placements unlock access to paid client briefs.
Paid client briefs have no playlist. Those tracks come straight to us.
